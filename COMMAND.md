# Command: kill-two-birds-with-one-stone

**Say it like:** "Grok, I need you to kill two birds with one stone."

## What happens next

1. Grok reads this entire repo (README.md + this file + any lessons).
2. Grok parses Perry's current needs from the conversation.
3. Grok runs the Playbook in README.md.
4. Grok reports back with either:
   - A single combined stop (best case), or
   - An honest "no combo found" + best individual options.
5. Grok routes Perry to the chosen stop.

## Do not

- Do not skip reading the repo.
- Do not guess at hours or locations.
- Do not recommend closed places.
- Do not over-explain. Perry wants the answer, then the drive.
